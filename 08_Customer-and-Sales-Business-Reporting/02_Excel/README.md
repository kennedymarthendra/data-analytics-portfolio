# 📊 Microsoft Excel Reports

This folder documents the Microsoft Excel workbooks developed to analyze customer behavior and sales performance during my internship.

The original Excel files contain confidential company information and therefore are **not included** in this repository.

---

# 📁 Workbook Overview

The project consists of three business reporting workbooks:

## 📄 Customer Reporting

Provides an overview of customer activity, purchasing trends, and customer growth across multiple years.

Key reports include:

- Customer Summary
- Customer Growth
- Customer Trends
- Cohort Analysis

---

## 📄 Customer Retention Analysis

Analyzes customer loyalty and repeat purchase behavior.

Key reports include:

- Customer Retention
- Returning Customers
- Top Customers
- Retention Performance

---

## 📄 Product Sales Reporting

Evaluates product and treatment sales performance.

Key reports include:

- Product Sales Summary
- Top Products
- Product Performance
- Revenue Analysis

---
