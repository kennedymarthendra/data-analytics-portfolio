# 📊 Customer & Sales Business Reporting

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a collection of business reports developed using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze customer behavior, customer retention, and product sales performance. The reports were designed to support business monitoring by transforming raw operational data into meaningful insights for strategic decision-making.

The project focuses on analyzing customer transactions and sales records from **Marvee Clinic Bogor** between **2021 and 2025**, providing comprehensive reporting on customer growth, purchasing behavior, retention, cohort performance, and product sales.

> **Note:** All confidential customer and company information has been anonymized or removed to maintain data privacy.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Business data was distributed across multiple operational records, making it difficult to monitor customer growth, evaluate retention performance, identify high-value customers, and assess product sales.

Management required an organized reporting solution that could:

- Monitor customer growth over multiple years.
- Evaluate customer retention and repeat purchase behavior.
- Analyze customer cohorts.
- Identify top-performing customers.
- Measure product and treatment sales performance.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.
