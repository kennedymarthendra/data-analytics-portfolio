## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through:

- Sales KPI summary
- Daily sales trend analysis
- Store location comparison
- Product category performance
- Top-selling products
- Weekday vs Weekend sales distribution
- Hourly sales heatmap for identifying peak business hours

The image offers a quick overview of the dashboard without requiring Power BI Desktop.
