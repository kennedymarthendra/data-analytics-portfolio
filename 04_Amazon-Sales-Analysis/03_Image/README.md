## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through:

- Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales KPI
- Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales KPI
- Products Sold Summary
- Customer Reviews Summary
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Weekly Sales Trend
- Product Category Performance
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Top 5 Products by Customer Reviews
- Interactive Product Category and Quarter Filters

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor product performance, identify high-performing categories, evaluate customer engagement through reviews, and support data-driven sales strategies.
